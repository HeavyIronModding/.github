## Welcome!

We're a community of modders for the games developed by Heavy Iron Studios, such as 
Scooby-Doo: Night of 100 Frights, Battle for Bikini Bottom and SpongeBob's Truth or Square.

### Where can I find mods?
Check out our list of mods on the wiki here: https://heavyironmodding.org/wiki/Mods

### How can I create mods?
For games using the "EvilEngine" (6th generation consoles), check out Industrial Park, 
created by igorseabra4. You'll want to check out the Essentials Series on our wiki for 
information on how to get started, at https://heavyironmodding.org/. For help, feel free 
to ask in the #help channel in our Discord server

### Do you host any modding events?
We host regular modding challenges in our Discord, where we encourage people to create
mods based on a theme, with deadlines of 2-3 months. We sometimes host Modding Jams, 
with shorter deadlines but submissions are judged and receive prizes! 

### How can I contribute?
Feel free to open a pull request on our repositories to contribute to our repositories.
